I BEGIN
WITH A question.
THEN I
build.

I study Games at the University of Utah, with minors in Psychology and Cognitive Science. Across independently built prototypes, 13-person game production and research assistantships, I investigate how interfaces structure attention, information and decisions.

My work connects HCI, learning technologies and cognitive research. I am interested in interfaces that offer interpretable feedback, adaptive learning environments and research-informed experiences—without assuming all users perceive information the same way.

Living in South Korea, Singapore, Saudi Arabia, Germany and the United States has shaped my awareness that different people bring different expectations and perspectives to the same experience. I carry that awareness into research participation, design decisions and collaborative work.
